Remove a job, or stop it watching a folder

Usage: aireg job remove <job> [--watch <folder>...]
Aliases: aireg job rm

Options:
      --watch <folder>  Stop watching this folder and keep the job (repeatable)

Learn more:
  Run `aireg --help` for the global options (--json, --verbose, -q, --color, --no-color, --log-level, -y).
  Docs: https://atlanai.github.io/ai-registry/reference/job/
