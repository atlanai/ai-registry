Sign in to your registries, and manage the accounts aireg uses

Usage: aireg auth [<action>] [<registry>] [options]

Actions:
  login   Sign in to a registry
  list    List the accounts aireg signs in with (alias: ls)
  logout  Sign out, keeping the account
  remove  Delete an account (alias: rm)

Examples:
  $ aireg auth login acme  sign in to the acme registry

Learn more:
  Run `aireg auth <action> --help` for an action's options.
  Run `aireg --help` for the global options (--json, --verbose, -q, --color, --no-color, --log-level, -y).
  Docs: https://atlanai.github.io/ai-registry/reference/auth/
